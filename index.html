About Us

Building reliable products across mobile, web, and backend

We are BaDi Systems, a product engineering team focused on delivering fast, scalable digital products for startups and growing businesses.

Our frontend stack is built for cross-platform delivery with Flutter, Jetpack Compose, SwiftUI, and Jaspr, allowing us to ship consistent user experiences across iOS, Android, and web.

On the backend, we build robust services using Go, PostgreSQL, and Docker, with an emphasis on clean architecture, performance, and reliable production deployments.

We also support students through mentoring, consultations, and structured guidance for essays, coursework, thesis projects, and lab assignments, helping them understand requirements and deliver stronger academic results.
